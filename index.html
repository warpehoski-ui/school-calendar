School Calendar by Grade

Select grades, then generate a calendar URL. The URL updates automatically when the school calendar changes.

How to add it to a calendar

Google Calendar: on a computer (or the desktop site on a phone), next to "Other calendars" click +, choose "From URL", and paste the URL. Google refreshes subscribed calendars slowly, often several hours to a day.

iPhone: Settings > Calendar > Accounts > Add Account > Other > Add Subscribed Calendar, then paste the URL.

Outlook: Add calendar > Subscribe from web, then paste the URL.
